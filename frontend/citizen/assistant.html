Flood Preparedness Checklist
  • Move critical electronics, documents, and valuables to upper floors.
  • Store clean drinking water in clean bottles for at least 3 days.
  • Keep emergency lighting (flashlights, candles, matches) in a dry bag.
  • Turn off the main electrical switch and gas valve if water enters your house.
  • Keep a bag packed with dry foods, first-aid kits, and essential medications.
Cyclone Preparedness Checklist
  • Inspect roof structures and fasten loose sheets.
  • Keep windows securely shut and board them up if wind speeds exceed 90km/h.
  • Charge mobile phones, power banks, and flashlights to 100% capacity.
  • Clear surrounding drains of leaves and debris to prevent clogging.
  • Stay indoors away from windows until the storm has completely passed.
Emergency Kit — What to Carry
  • Bottled water (3 liters per person per day).
  • Non-perishable food items (biscuits, energy bars, dry fruit).
  • Personal hygiene items (wet wipes, hand sanitizer, soap).
  • First-aid kit (bandages, antiseptic ointment, pain relievers, prescription medicines).
  • Photocopies of identification documents (Aadhaar, passport, ration card) in a waterproof plastic sleeve.
